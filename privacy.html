Health Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

Health Tracker is a personal, read-only application developed for the account owner's personal use. The application accesses the owner's Google Health data to allow the owner to view and analyze their own health information.

2. Google Health Data

The application may access Google Health data, including profile, settings, activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, and nutrition information, solely at the direction of the account owner.

3. How Data Is Used

Google Health data is used only to provide personal health data access, analysis, and related functionality requested by the account owner. The data is not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell Google Health data. Google Health data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the functionality explicitly requested by the account owner.

5. Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored using access-controlled storage mechanisms. Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information from unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner may stop the application's access to Google Health data by revoking the application's Google account access. Stored application data and credentials can also be deleted from the application's storage.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Health Tracker is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Information provided by the application is for personal informational purposes only.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the Google OAuth application.
